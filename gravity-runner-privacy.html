Gravity Runner Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DMTools.fun LLC handles information in connection with the Gravity Runner mobile game.

1. Who We Are

Gravity Runner is published by DMTools.fun LLC. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at admin@dmtools.fun.

2. Information We May Collect

Gravity Runner is designed to be played without creating an account. We do not require players to submit names, usernames, messages, or other account information to use the game.

Depending on the services enabled in the app, the game or its service providers may process limited technical information such as:

  • device and app identifiers
  • advertising identifiers
  • app version, device type, operating system, language, and basic diagnostic information
  • gameplay and usage events needed to operate, improve, measure, or support the app
  • purchase or entitlement status where applicable, such as ad-removal ownership or restore checks

3. Advertising, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Gravity Runner may use third-party services for app-store distribution, advertising, consent management, purchase or entitlement verification, analytics, and crash or performance diagnostics. The exact providers and data practices depend on the store build and enabled SDKs. Those providers may process device-level or app-level information under their own privacy policies.

If ads are enabled in the version you use, advertising providers may process IP address, advertising or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, consent status, device information, and diagnostic data to deliver, limit, secure, and measure ads. Personalized advertising is used only where permitted by applicable law, store policy, user age or audience treatment, and consent choices.

Store operators may process purchase, refund, and entitlement information. Gravity Runner does not receive full payment-card details from an app store.

4. How We Use Information

  • operate and maintain Gravity Runner
  • deliver and measure advertising
  • support purchases, restores, or entitlements where available
  • diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance issues
  • improve gameplay, balance, usability, and reliability
  • comply with legal obligations and protect the app from abuse or misuse

5. Sharing

We do not sell player-submitted personal information through Gravity Runner. Information may be processed or shared with service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, app store, or payment-related providers, only as needed to support the app and related services.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, and improve Gravity Runner, or as required by law. Some local game data may remain on your device until you delete the app or clear app data.

7. Your Choices And Requests

  • You can stop future app activity by uninstalling the app and can remove local game data by clearing app storage.
  • You can reset or limit advertising identifiers and manage applicable ad or consent choices through device, store, and advertising-provider settings.
  • Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning information controlled by DMTools.fun LLC.
  • You may submit a privacy request to admin@dmtools.fun. We may verify identity where reasonably necessary and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children

Gravity Runner is offered as a general-audience game and does not provide account creation, chat, messaging, or user-generated-content features. Device-level information may nevertheless be processed by enabled advertising, store, consent, analytics, or diagnostic SDKs.

If a store build is directed to children or a mixed audience that includes children, DMTools.fun LLC will apply required age treatment and configure SDKs to comply with applicable children’s privacy and store policies, including restrictions on personalized advertising, advertising identifiers, and precise location. Parents or guardians may contact us to ask questions or request deletion of information, if any, controlled by DMTools.fun LLC.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation of the app, but no method of transmission, storage, or processing is completely secure.

10. International Processing

Depending on the services used to operate Gravity Runner, information may be processed in countries other than your own.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the effective date above and publish the revised version at https://dmtools.fun.
