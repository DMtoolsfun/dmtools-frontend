Understand the problem first

Three flags. One practical plan.

A useful review tells you what matters and why. Each finding includes its location, evidence, proposed fix, completion check, and price or investigation requirement.

BROKEN / BLOCKING

It fails or blocks your launch.

Failed features, incorrect results, verified security defects, or issues blocking the agreed launch. We distinguish reproduced failures from source-only findings.

MISSING

A launch essential is missing.

Requirements for your agreed launch, such as production configuration, payment confirmation, or useful error handling.

MESSY

It works, but needs cleanup.

Confusing navigation, duplicate code, inconsistent styling, or maintenance problems. Cleanup gets an honest place in the queue.

Flags describe the finding. Priority determines the order. Unconfirmed concerns are marked “Needs verification.” A scoped review is not a comprehensive security audit.

Before you start

A few straight answers.

Can you work on a website built with AI?

Yes. We first check the stack, repository, and problem to establish whether the project fits. Unsupported stacks or larger work receive an honest scope decision before payment.

Do I have to buy every fix?

No. Choose individual items or the complete agreed plan. Some items depend on others; those dependencies are explained before you approve work.

Does a full rescue guarantee my business will succeed?

The deliverable is the agreed functioning product and its acceptance checks. Sales, traffic, search rankings, and profitability are not guaranteed.

What happens to my code and accounts?

You retain your existing code and accounts. Access is arranged securely for the agreed work. Deployment ownership, deliverables, and access removal are included in the handover scope.

What does the $49 review credit cover?

The $49 review fee is deducted once from subsequently purchased repairs for the same reviewed project. The written quote records the credit; unused credit is not a cash refund.
