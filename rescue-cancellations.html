Cancellations & refunds

This policy covers Website Rescue reviews and repairs. The inquiry form places no order and takes no payment. Non-waivable consumer cancellation and remedy rights always take precedence.

1. Before work is ordered

A free inquiry or check may be withdrawn without a fee. Before a paid review or repair starts, we agree in writing on scope, price, payment schedule, deliverables, applicable policies, and any cancellation/start arrangements. No charge is authorized by submitting the form.

2. Canceling paid work

Contact rescue@dmtools.fun and identify the quote/invoice and work to stop. Before work begins, prepaid service fees are refundable. After work begins, subject to applicable consumer rights, payment is limited to authorized work actually performed under the agreed price allocation, plus separately approved nonrecoverable third-party costs to the extent lawfully chargeable. Unearned prepaid fees are refunded. We explain the calculation rather than applying a blanket “no refunds” rule.

For fixed-price milestones, the scope should allocate prices or a cancellation calculation in advance. We will not invent a new hourly rate after cancellation. If the amount is disputed, we provide the work/approval record and address the dispute before asserting an unsupported charge.

3. Review fee and repair credit

A completed review is a separate deliverable, not a promise that repairs will be purchased. The $49 introductory review credit applies once toward paid repairs for the same reviewed project as recorded in the quote. If the completed review was supplied as agreed, declining further work does not by itself require a refund. This does not limit remedies for nonperformance or defective services, or statutory cancellation rights.

4. If we cannot complete the work

We notify you, stop unauthorized further charges, provide usable completed materials within the agreed rights/access arrangements, and refund unearned prepaid amounts. Any proposed substitution or revised scope needs your agreement. If work fails agreed acceptance checks, contact us for correction and any refund or other remedy required by the agreement or law. We do not require you to waive legal rights to raise a problem.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where feasible. We communicate the amount, basis, and expected processing time and comply with applicable deadlines. Provider/bank posting times may vary. No automatic subscription or recurring service is created through this site.

6. EEA and UK consumers: distance-contract cancellation

Where applicable consumer distance-selling law covers your contract, you generally have 14 days from entering a service contract to withdraw without giving a reason. Before ordering, we supply the required contract information, trader contact details, cancellation instructions and any required model form in a durable format, such as email. These protections may differ for business-to-business work.

We will not start covered work during that period merely because you sent an inquiry, checked the form’s data-processing box, or paid. Starting early requires your separate express request and required information/acknowledgments. If you validly request an early start and then cancel, a proportionate amount for services supplied may be payable where legally permitted. Loss of the withdrawal right after full performance requires the applicable advance express consent and acknowledgment; it is not automatic when work starts. Missing required information may extend statutory cancellation periods.

7. Other regional rights

US state and other applicable local consumer protections remain available. A Nevada governing-law clause does not waive mandatory rights elsewhere. Tell us the relevant jurisdiction before ordering if special disclosures or a local cancellation process are required. We must assess those requirements before accepting the work; this page is not certification of compliance in every country.
